# Provisional Graph War audio reuse (V0.5.7)

This build borrows all **currently playable** music tracks and all three combat SFX from [Graph War](https://github.com/GuyT1225/graph-war), pending a final Orbital Defender sound identity.

## SFX, qubodup — Tiny Naval Battle Sounds Set (CC0)
- `fire_heavy.wav` — GunShotGverb, used at differing playback speeds/levels for weapon launches.
- `impact_pen.wav` — ExplosionMetalGverb, used for target destruction.
- `water_explode.opus` — WaterSurfaceExplosion01, used for target damage and large missed blasts.
- Origin: https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-naval-battle-sounds-set
- Graph War provenance: https://github.com/GuyT1225/graph-war/blob/main/assets/audio/README.md

The SFX are streamed from raw Graph War repository URLs, **not copied into Orbital Defender yet**. Network failures can prevent samples from sounding; existing synthesized SFX remain enabled as a fallback.

Music uses original remote OpenGameArt URLs with existing procedural fallback behavior. Tracks are assigned provisionally to Tactical and Arcade waves. Source: https://github.com/GuyT1225/graph-war/blob/main/assets/audio/MUSIC_README.md

**Exploration Theme** (Cleyton Kauffman) is listed in Graph War only as a preferred future anchor with no playable URL. It is not included in this runtime until its audio is actually obtained and verified.

Do not claim that these assets are self-hosted. Before release, review sources/availability, download and package licensed audio locally, and establish consistent mastering/mixing.
